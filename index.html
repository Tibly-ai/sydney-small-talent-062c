About Sydney

Based in New York City, Sydney Small represents actors, models, and performers across film, television, theater, and commercial work. Her approach is personal, discerning, and results-driven — she takes on a select roster of talent so every client receives her full attention.

From contract negotiation to career strategy, Sydney partners with each client to build a body of work that reflects their ambition and craft.
